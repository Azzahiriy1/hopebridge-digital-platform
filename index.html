Learn to protect and plan your money

Short, practical reminders for households and small businesses.

🧾

Keep records

Write down every sale and expense, even small ones. Separate business money from personal spending where possible.

🔐

Avoid fraud

Never share your PIN, OTP or password. Verify offers through official websites and never pay an unknown person to secure a grant.

🎯

Plan savings

Choose a realistic target, save regularly and review your progress. Do not borrow unless the repayment plan is clear.
